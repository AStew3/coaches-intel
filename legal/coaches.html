For coaches

Effective November 11, 2026 · Applies to RecruitDirect and Fair Catch

RecruitDirect helps high school athletes and their families find the right college coaches and reach out about their own recruiting. Here's what that means for you as a coach.

Where your information comes from

The directory lists names, titles, schools, work email addresses, office phone numbers and public social handles of coaches and recruiting staff, taken from public sources such as your school's athletics staff directory. We don't buy contact lists or collect personal phone numbers or home addresses.

Who sends the messages

Messages are written and sent by the athlete or their parent, from their own email, phone or X account. RecruitDirect doesn't send marketing to coaches. Athletes are told that NCAA, NAIA and NJCAA rules may limit when you can respond, and that a coach not replying is expected.

Film and profile links

When you open an athlete's film or profile link, the athlete can see that the link was opened and when. We don't follow you around other websites or show you ads.

Correct or remove your listing, or stop messages

  • To correct your title, email or school, or to remove your entry from the directory, email drumaine@gmail.com from your school email address, or include a link to your staff page. We'll act within 10 business days.
  • To stop messages from a particular athlete, reply to that athlete and ask them to stop. They're required to honor it and can mark you Do not contact, which leaves you out of their bulk sends.
  • To report spam or misuse of the Service, email us with the message and we'll investigate and may suspend the sender.

Not affiliated

RecruitDirect is operated by OPERIS. It is not affiliated with the NCAA, NAIA, NJCAA, any conference or your institution.
