Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026 · Applies to RecruitDirect and Fair Catch

The short version. We collect what you put in the app, what's needed to sign you in, and what you send, so the Service works. We don't sell personal information, we don't use it for targeted advertising, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers. You can see, fix, download or delete your information by emailing us.

1. Who we are

OPERIS ("we," "us") operates RecruitDirect and Fair Catch (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and your choices. It works with our Terms of Service.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: email address, and mobile number if you choose text sign-in; your role (athlete, parent or guardian); and for parents, the athletes you manage.
  • Athlete profile: name, high school, graduation year, sport and position, height and weight, stats, academic details you choose to add, photo, highlight film links or uploaded video, social handles, location (city and state), and notes.
  • Messages and recruiting activity: the coaches you message, the channel (email, text or X), message text and subject, when you sent it, replies you paste in, follow-up dates, coach status labels (such as "Do not contact"), offers and visit notes.
  • Payments: if you buy a plan, Stripe collects your payment details. We receive only limited information such as your plan, payment status and a customer ID, not your full card number.
  • Support: what you send us when you contact us.

Information collected automatically

  • Sign-in and security: a sign-in token stored on your device (in local storage, a cookie and IndexedDB) so you stay signed in, plus basic records of sign-ins and code requests to prevent abuse. These are strictly necessary; we don't use advertising or analytics cookies.
  • App usage: limited records of actions in the app (for example, that a bulk send was started or a template used) and daily sending counts, used to run features and limits.
  • Film and profile link views: when someone opens an athlete's film or profile link, or scans a camp QR code, we record which link was opened and when, and, if the link was sent to a specific coach, which coach. This is how the app shows athletes "who opened the film." We don't use tracking pixels to follow people across other websites.
  • Device data: like any website, our hosting providers receive your IP address and browser information when you load the Service. We don't collect precise location.

Information about coaches

The coach directory contains names, titles, schools, work email addresses, office phone numbers and public social handles of college coaches and staff, collected from public sources such as school athletics staff directories. See For coaches to correct or remove an entry.

3. How we use information

  • to provide the Service: sign you in, build and show the athlete profile and film page, fill in and send your messages, keep your message history, and show views and replies;
  • to send sign-in codes, account notices and, if you turn them on, reminders;
  • to generate AI drafts and answers when you use those features;
  • to keep the Service secure, prevent spam and abuse, and enforce sending limits and our Terms;
  • to fix problems and improve the Service; and
  • to comply with law and respond to valid legal requests.

We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral (targeted) advertising, or show ads in the Service.

4. How information is shared

  • With the people you choose: coaches you message receive your message and anything it contains or links to; anyone with your film or profile link can view it; parents or guardians and athletes you link can see and edit the shared account.
  • With service providers who process information for us under contract and only to provide their service: Google (hosting, data storage, Google sign-in and sending email), Twilio (sign-in text messages), X Corp. (only if you connect your X account, to send and read your direct messages), Stripe (payments), Anthropic (AI features; the text you ask about and relevant profile details are sent to generate a response), and GitHub (hosting the install pages). Your device may also request school logos from Google's public favicon service and school names from the U.S. Department of Education College Scorecard.
  • For legal reasons: to comply with law, legal process or government requests, or to protect the rights, safety and property of users, the public or OPERIS.
  • Business transfers: if OPERIS is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information may transfer under this Policy's protections, and we'll tell you.

5. Children and teens

  • The Service is not for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have, we'll delete it. Parents who believe their child under 13 used the Service can email us.
  • Many users are high school athletes aged 13 to 17. For them we collect only what the Service needs, don't sell or share their information for advertising, don't show targeted ads, don't collect precise location, and require a parent or guardian to agree to the Terms and to make any purchase.
  • A parent or guardian can ask to review, correct, download or delete their teen's information, or change what is shared, by emailing drumaine@gmail.com from the email on the account, or through the parent tools in the app.

6. How long we keep information

We keep account and profile information while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or de-identify your information within 30 days, except for limited records we must keep for legal, security, tax or fraud-prevention reasons, and copies in backups that are overwritten on a regular schedule. Messages you already sent stay with the people who received them.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), signed sign-in tokens, one-time codes and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in private and tell us about any suspected unauthorized access.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can view and edit most of your information in the app, and remove film, photos or messages at any time.
  • You can ask us to access, correct, download (portability) or delete your personal information, or to tell you what we have, by emailing drumaine@gmail.com. We'll verify the request comes from you (or your parent or authorized agent) and answer within 45 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example Texas, California, Colorado, Virginia and other states with privacy laws), you may have these rights by law. We don't sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising or profiling with legal effects. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our decision; if you're not satisfied, you may contact your state attorney general.
  • We won't treat you differently for using your privacy rights.
  • We honor Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out of sale or sharing; since we don't sell or share for advertising, nothing else changes.
  • Texts: reply STOP to any sign-in text to stop them.

9. Where information is processed

The Service is run from, and information is stored in, the United States. It is intended for users in the United States.

10. Changes

We'll post any changes here and update the effective date. If a change is material, we'll show a notice in the app or email you before it takes effect.
